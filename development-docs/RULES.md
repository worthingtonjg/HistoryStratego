# Rule profile

Reference: Hasbro's official full-size rulebook:
https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/Stratego.PDF

Specialist/retail movement language cross-checked against the licensed PlayMonster rules:
https://www.playmonster.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/06/7471_Classic_English_Rules.pdf
That latter product contains 30 pieces; its reduced setup/counts are NOT used.

Per side: Flag 1; Bomb 6; Spy 1; Scout 8; Miner 5; Sergeant 4; Lieutenant 4; Captain 4; Major 3; Colonel 2; General 1; Marshal 1 (40 total). Modern labels run Spy 1 through Marshal 10.

The board uses 100 squares and blocked lake cells 42,43,46,47,52,53,56,57 (zero-based row-major). Each formation occupies four home rows. Red Confederate forces move first; blue Union forces second.

One orthogonal square per ordinary move. Scouts can cross clear straight paths and attack at distance, never jump. Bombs and Flags cannot move. Higher combat rank wins, equals both disappear; only an attacking Spy defeats Marshal, and only Miner defeats a Bomb. Flag capture or inability to make a legal move loses.

Classroom house rule: repeated back-and-forth moves are allowed; the retail two-square repetition restriction is deliberately omitted. Tournament “more-square” chasing adjudication is not implemented. No shortened classroom mode is used.

Faction names and piece encounters are fictional game framing. No claim is made that Marshal/Spy/Bomb labels describe historically exact American Civil War units.
